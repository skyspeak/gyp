# 20. Podcast guest appearances

## Finding shows

Search Apple Podcasts and Spotify for:

- `college admissions`
- `paying for college`
- `gap year`
- `financial aid`
- `personal finance for young adults`

Pick shows that have had guests in the last three months and at least a few
episodes on college costs or decisions. Listen to one episode before
pitching, and mention something specific from it.

Don't pitch shows sponsored by a gap year operator. The interview would have
to avoid your whole point.

## The pitch

**Subject:** `Guest idea: the fellowships that ended and the gap years that pay`

```
Hi {{host}},

I enjoyed your episode on {{episode topic}}, especially {{specific point}}.

I'd like to suggest myself as a guest. I run a free, non-commercial index of
gap year and post-grad programs, and the data turned up two things your
listeners probably haven't heard:

1. Federally backed fellowships that students plan careers around have
   stopped, and university pages still advertise them. The Payne Fellowship
   was terminated in February 2025. Pickering and Rangel postponed their 2026
   cycles.

2. Families mostly hear about gap years that cost up to $75,000, while 230
   programs still running pay participants instead.

I take no commissions and nothing on the site is sponsored, so I can talk
about prices candidly. One-page data sheet attached.

Gautam
https://gyp-psi.vercel.app/?ref=pod-{{show}}
```

Attach `03-data-sheet.html`, printed to PDF.

## Talking points

Say these numbers exactly. They're from the catalog on 15 September 2026;
recheck before recording.

- **230** gap year and post-grad programs still running pay the participant.
  **89** charge.
- Conservation: **7** charge, **40** pay. Teaching abroad: **5** charge, **29** pay.
- Travel and study, and outdoor, are where most charge. Outdoor has almost no
  paid option: **15** charge, **4** pay.
- Prices: postgraduate year at a boarding school up to **$75,000**; EF Gap
  Year about **$43,750**.
- Pay: Teach For America **$32,000–$72,000** a year; NIH postbac research
  **$46,100–$59,300** a year.
- Payne Fellowship: terminated **27 February 2025**. Pickering and Rangel:
  **2026 cycles postponed**. Mitchell Scholarship: **paused since March 2024**.

## Things not to say

- Don't name the universities whose pages list closed fellowships. The
  pattern is the story; naming offices burns relationships you want.
- Don't say every category has a paid option. Outdoor mostly doesn't.
- Don't round 230 up to "hundreds that pay". The precise number is more
  believable.
