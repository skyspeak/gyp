# 13. Library research guides

LibGuides are curated resource pages made by school and college librarians.
Gap year guides already exist, for example at Milton Academy
(libguides.milton.edu), Antioch College and Cypress College.

## Finding them

Search Google for:

```
site:libguides.com "gap year"
site:libguides.com "service year" OR "gap year programs"
site:libguides.com "fellowships" "Payne" OR "Mitchell Scholarship"
inurl:libguides "gap year" high school
```

Each guide shows its owner, usually a named librarian with a contact button,
in the sidebar.

## Before writing

Open the guide and note, honestly:

- anything listed that has closed or paused (check the program's own site)
- programs that charge money listed without the price
- dead links

If none of that applies, still write, just without the correction line.

## The email

**Subject:** `A free resource for your gap year guide`

```
Hi {{first_name}},

I came across your {{guide name}} guide. It's one of the more useful gap year
pages I've found.

[Only if true: One small thing: {{program}} on the guide {{has closed /
paused selection / no longer loads}}, per {{official source}}.]

I maintain a free, non-commercial index of gap year and post-grad programs
that might be worth adding. It separates programs that pay participants
from the ones that charge them, lists the price where there is one, and
removes programs as they close. Nothing on it is sponsored.

https://gyp-psi.vercel.app/?ref=lib-{{schoolcode}}

There's also a map by country, and a free CSV of the verified programs if
students are doing research with it:
https://gyp-psi.vercel.app/api/dataset.csv

Gautam
```
