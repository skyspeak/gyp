# 18. December deferral season

Early decision and early action results arrive in mid-December. That week,
three groups start asking about gap years at once: admitted students who
aren't ready, deferred students weighing options, and students who didn't get
in and want a year to regroup.

Most colleges publish their own ED release date. Check two or three large ones
in early December, and shift this plan to that week.
