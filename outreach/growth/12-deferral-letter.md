## Reddit replies (December)

In r/ApplyingToCollege and r/gapyear, when someone asks how to defer:

**"How do I ask to defer?"**
```
Check your college's deferral policy first. Search their admissions site for
"defer". Most want a written request, and many want to approve a specific
plan: what you'll do, when, and confirmation you won't enroll somewhere else
for credit.

A plan with a named program and dates is much easier to approve than
"travel and work". There's a free letter template here if it helps:
https://gyp-psi.vercel.app/deferral-letter?ref=deferral-reddit
```

**"What should I even do for a gap year?"**
```
Worth knowing: most gap year programs that advertise charge money, but
plenty pay a stipend instead. Conservation corps, AmeriCorps, teaching
assistant programs abroad. A paid program also makes the deferral request
easier, because it's a concrete plan.
```
Only add the link if they ask for a list.
