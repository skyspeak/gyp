# 9. Video scripts

Five short videos, 25–45 seconds each, for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube
Shorts. No filming. Every shot is a screen recording of the site on your phone.

## Setup, once

1. On your iPhone: Settings → Control Center → add Screen Recording. On
   Android, use the built-in screen recorder.
2. Open https://gyp-psi.vercel.app/map in your phone browser and turn on
   light mode (it records more clearly).
3. Record each shot listed below separately. Keep taps slow.
4. Edit in CapCut or the platform's own editor: add the on-screen text,
   then record the voiceover.
5. Bio link, one per platform:
   - TikTok: `https://gyp-psi.vercel.app/map?ref=video-tiktok`
   - Instagram: `https://gyp-psi.vercel.app/map?ref=video-ig`
   - YouTube: `https://gyp-psi.vercel.app/map?ref=video-yt`

Post one every three or four days.

## Video 1: The map

**On-screen hook (first 2 seconds):** `55 countries you can do a gap year in`

**Shots:** the full map → tap Japan → scroll the list → tap Australia → scroll.

**Voiceover:**
```
There are 55 countries where an American can actually do a gap year, and I
mapped all of them. Tap a country, and you see every program there, with what
it pays or what it costs. Japan has a teaching program that pays over four
million yen a year. Nothing on this map is sponsored. Link's in my bio.
```

---

## Video 2: The price

**Hook:** `A gap year can cost $75,000. Here's the other option.`

**Shots:** catalog filtered to "You pay" → a $43,750 program → switch to
"Pays you" → scroll a long list.

**Voiceover:**
```
A postgraduate year at a boarding school can cost seventy-five thousand
dollars. EF Gap Year is about forty-four thousand. But there are 230 gap year
programs that pay you instead: conservation corps, teaching abroad, AmeriCorps.
Most people just never hear about them, because they don't have ad budgets.
```

---

## Video 3: Conservation

**Hook:** `7 conservation gap years charge you. 40 pay you.`

**Shots:** catalog, filter Conservation → scroll programs showing "Pays you" badges.

**Voiceover:**
```
If you want to spend a year outside doing conservation work, don't pay for
it. Seven programs charge a fee. Forty pay you a stipend. Same kind of work,
opposite cash flow.
```

---

## Video 4: The honest one

**Hook:** `The one gap year you'll probably have to pay for`

**Shots:** catalog, filter Outdoor → programs with "You pay" badges.

**Voiceover:**
```
Most kinds of gap year have plenty of versions that pay you. Outdoor and
wilderness semesters are the exception. Fifteen charge, only four pay. So if that's your
dream year, budget for it. Everything else, check the paid options first.
```

---

## Video 5: The closures

**Hook:** `These fellowships are still on college websites. They're not taking applications.`

**Shots:** a program page marked shut down or paused → the map.

**Voiceover:**
```
The Payne Fellowship was terminated in February 2025. The Mitchell
Scholarship has been paused since 2024. Pickering and Rangel postponed their
2026 cycles. Some college fellowship pages still list them as open. Check
before you spend a month on an application.
```

**Don't** show or name any university page in this video. The point is the
pattern, not embarrassing an office you might want to work with.
