# 16. Alumni networks of paying programs

Alumni of programs that pay get asked "how did you get that?" all the time.
A neutral page to send people to saves them the answer.

## Where to find them

| Network | Size | Where to find chapter contacts |
|---|---|---|
| JET Programme alumni (JETAA USA) | 19 US chapters | usjetaa.org → U.S. JETAA Chapters |
| National Peace Corps Association | 145+ affiliate groups | peacecorpsconnect.org → Affiliate Groups Directory |

Peace Corps volunteers receive a living allowance and a readjustment payment
at the end of service, so it belongs in the "pays" group. JET is salaried.

Also look for alumni groups of AmeriCorps programs and conservation corps on
LinkedIn and Facebook.

## The email

**Subject:** `A page to send people who ask about {{program}}`

```
Hi {{first_name}},

I run a free, non-commercial index of gap year and post-grad programs, and
{{program}} is on it as one that pays participants rather than charging them:

https://gyp-psi.vercel.app/programs/{{slug}}?ref=alumni-{{group}}

I imagine your members get asked by students and recent grads how to do
what they did. If it's useful, this is a neutral place to send them: it
lists {{program}} next to similar paid options, with what each pays and
the official links.

If anything on {{program}}'s page is out of date, I'd be grateful for a
correction, since alumni know it better than anyone.

No sponsorships, and nothing on the site is paid placement.

Gautam
```

## Program slugs

- JET: `jet-alt`
- Peace Corps: `peace-corps`

The correction offer at the end is the part that tends to get replies.
Alumni like being asked what they know.
