# 8. Parent groups price check

For large Facebook groups about college admissions and paying for college.
Search Facebook for terms like "college admissions parents", "paying for
college" and "gap year parents", and join two or three big, active ones.

## Rules for yourself

- Read each group's rules. Most ban promotional posts. Many allow a link in a
  helpful comment, and some don't allow links at all.
- Never start a post about the site. Only answer questions that are already there.
- Attach the image (`08-price-card.html`, screenshot it) instead of a link. Add
  the link only if someone asks where the numbers come from.
- Keep it to 15 minutes a day. More than that turns into spam.

## The image

Open `08-price-card.html` in a browser and take a screenshot of the card. It's
1080×1080, which Facebook and Instagram both display at full size.

## Replies

**"Is [program] worth $30,000?"**
```
Depends what you want the year to do, but worth knowing before you pay: for
most kinds of gap year there are more programs that pay a stipend than
programs that charge. In conservation it's roughly 40 that pay against 7 that
charge. Teaching abroad is 29 against 5.

The exceptions are outdoor semesters, where there's hardly any paid option
(4 pay, 15 charge), and travel-and-study programs, where more charge than pay.

Might be worth comparing before committing. [attach image]
```

**"My kid wants to defer. What do they actually do for a year?"**
```
We looked into this. The options that surprised us were the paid ones:
conservation corps, AmeriCorps programs, teaching assistant programs abroad.
They give a stipend or wage, and some come with an education award too.

The expensive programs are just the ones that advertise most. [attach image]
```

**"Where are those numbers from?"** (the only time you post the link)
```
A free index that sorts gap year programs by whether they pay or charge.
Nothing on it is sponsored: https://gyp-psi.vercel.app/?ref=fb-price
```

## If a moderator objects

Delete the comment, apologize once, and don't repost it in that group.
