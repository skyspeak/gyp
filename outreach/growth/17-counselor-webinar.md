# 17. Counselor association webinar

NACAC has 23 regional affiliates, each running professional development
for college counselors. NACAC's own webinar guidance says sessions must focus
on educational content, and prefers panels that include a practicing
counselor. Build the session that way: education, not a product demo.

## Who to send it to

The professional development or programming chair of two or three
affiliates. Each affiliate lists its board on its own site (NACAC links to all
23 from its affiliates page). Start with affiliates in states where you have
a counselor contact who could co-present.

## The proposal

**Title:** Paid Gap Years: What Families Aren't Hearing, and the Fellowships
That Quietly Ended

**Format:** 45-minute webinar with 15 minutes of Q&A. Ideally co-presented
with a practicing counselor.

**Description (for the program listing):**
```
Families considering a gap year mostly hear about programs that charge,
some up to $75,000. Yet 230 gap year and post-grad programs still running pay
participants a stipend, wage or education award. This session gives
counselors a practical way to talk about gap years with families across
income levels: which kinds of year usually pay and which usually cost, how to
spot a program that has closed or paused, and how students can request a
deferral. Participants leave with a checklist and a free deadline calendar.
```

**Learning outcomes:**
1. Distinguish gap years that pay from ones that charge, by category.
2. Check whether a recommended program is still running before advising a student.
3. Help a student write a deferral request with a concrete plan.

## Session outline

| Min | Section | Content |

| 0–5 | The question families ask | "Is a gap year worth it?" And why the answer depends on which door they walk through |
