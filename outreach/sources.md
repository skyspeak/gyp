## Claimed in Email 1

**Donald M. Payne International Development Fellowship — terminated**
Terminated 27 February 2025 in connection with the shutdown of USAID programs.
Members of Congress, including Rep. LaMonica McIver writing to Secretary Rubio
in March 2025, called for reinstatement; as of August 2026 no cohort is being
recruited.
Catalog source: https://www.usf.edu/graduate-studies/funding/graduate-student-research-funding/payne-fellowship.aspx

**Thomas R. Pickering Foreign Affairs Fellowship — 2026 cycle postponed**
Postponed pending updates from the U.S. Department of State, no restart
announced as of August 2026. Onboarding of already-selected fellows was also
delayed and drew a congressional inquiry.
Source: https://apply.pickeringfellowship.org/

**Charles B. Rangel International Affairs Fellowship — 2026 cycle postponed**
Same posture as Pickering.
Source: https://apply.rangelprogram.org/

**George J. Mitchell Scholarship — selection paused**
The US-Ireland Alliance announced in March 2024 that it was pausing selection
of future classes. As of August 2026 the notice is still on the official
application process page and no new cycle is open.
Source: https://us-irelandalliance.org/mitchellscholarship/applicants/process
