# Dead link emails — schools, colleges and directories

Ready to proofread and send. One email per page that still points students at a
program that has closed or stopped taking applicants. The full list, with what
each page says and where its links land, is at `/deadlinks` (admin login).

## Before you send anything

- [ ] **Open the page the morning you send it.** Some of these were checked on
      15 September 2026 and some on the 17th. A correction that arrives after
      they already fixed it makes you look careless.
- [ ] Check the official source too — Pickering and Rangel could restart.
- [ ] Send one at a time from your own address. No mail merge, no BCC list.
- [ ] Never say "your page is wrong" in the subject line. The subject should
      name the program, so it routes to whoever owns that page.
- [ ] If they reply and fix it, thank them and stop. Do not pitch again.

A note on tone: these offices are understaffed and the pages are usually
orphaned, not neglected on purpose. Lead with the fact, give the source, and
make the link optional.
