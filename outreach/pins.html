Gap Year Pin Sheet

Ten pins, authored at Pinterest's 1000×1500. To export: print this page to PDF, which resets each pin to full size and puts one per page, then export the PDF pages as images. Screenshotting a tile works too, but a tile is 320px wide and Pinterest wants 1000, so printing is the route that keeps the quality.

Titles, descriptions and destination links for each pin are in pinterest.md. The ref codes are already in those links, so signups get attributed to the pin that produced them.
